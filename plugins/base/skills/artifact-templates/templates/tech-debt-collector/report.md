**Confidence Tier Definitions** (C1-C4 ascending):
- **C1 (Speculative/Lowest)**: Evidence incomplete or partially contradicted
- **C2 (Provisional)**: Supporting evidence but decision-critical test missing
- **C3 (Supported)**: Scope reasonably covered, no known contradiction
- **C4 (Well-Established/Highest)**: Evidence converges, claim survived refutation
